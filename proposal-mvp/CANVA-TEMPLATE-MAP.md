## Website-first order

The website should use the same content in a stronger client-decision order:

1. Personalised cover with the strongest 3D render
2. Remus walkthrough video
3. The client's acoustic problem and recommended treatment
4. Result figures: panel quantity, current and target RT60, expected echo reduction and main benefit
5. Project-specific 3D views and floor-plan placement
6. Matching product and installation references
7. Relevant before/after video proof
8. One or two space-matched case studies
9. Why Just Acoustics / credibility proof
10. What happens next
11. FAQ
12. Investment summary, embedded quote and downloadable files
13. Final acceptance or change-request action

## Project-specific fields

- Client, company, project name and space type
- Project date and proposal reference
- Problem and desired outcome
- 3D hero, overview, eye-level and close-up renders
- Walkthrough video
- Panel product, quantity, dimensions, thickness, finish and mounting
- Current RT60, target RT60 and expected reduction
- Main client benefit
- Product reference images
- Before/after examples selected by space type
- Case studies selected by space type
- Next-step timeline
- FAQ variations
- Quote summary, quote PDF and individual asset downloads

## Standard reusable content

- Who We Are
- Approved client logos
- Team roles
- Services overview
- Why Choose Us layout
- Contact details
- Standard commercial process

## Accuracy gates

- Do not reuse the template's example acoustic figures for a new client.
- Do not use a case study or before/after example from the wrong space type.
- Do not show product photos that differ from the quoted product.
- Verify 4.9 Google rating, 99% satisfaction and 100+ projects before publishing.
- Keep figures identical across the site, infographic cards, PDF and Zoho Books estimate.
